# UX Strategy — Restaurant & Menu (page-level)
_Status: Draft_

## Why this page exists

The social restaurant is the heart of De Harmonie. This page exists to answer one question fast — "what's on the menu this week?" — while also giving newcomers and family members enough context to understand how it works and feel welcome to come.

## User needs

| Audience | What they need |
|----------|----------------|
| **Returning visitor (senior)** | This week's menu, immediately. Price confirmation. |
| **Family member** | Which days, what time, how much, walk-in or reserve? Can I send my parent? |
| **New visitor** | Atmosphere and warmth — does this feel like a place for me? |

## The hidden tension

Returning visitors want the menu without scrolling. Newcomers need context before the menu means anything to them. The page must serve both: a short intro that doesn't block, then the menu front and center.

## Site objectives this page serves

1. **Answer the meal question** — the #1 most-searched content on the site
2. **Build trust for decision-makers** — families need to feel confident sending their parent
3. **Lower the shame barrier** — never feel institutional; lead with warmth and community

## Tone

Not "Diensten > Maaltijden > Weekmenu". More like: "Elke dag een warm middagmaal — kom gewoon binnen."
