**Synthesis:** Belgian buurtwerk/neighborhood center sites generally don't have dedicated volunteer pages. They rely on word-of-mouth and direct contact. A well-designed volunteer page is a meaningful differentiator for De Harmonie.

## Design patterns observed

- Photo-forward: real community photos build trust instantly
- Cards with icons: widely used for "why volunteer" sections on comparable nonprofit sites
- Minimal forms: 3-field max (name, email, message) is the norm for volunteer intake
- Reassurance copy: phrases like "geen verplichtingen" and "op jouw tempo" are effective at dissolving the over-commitment fear
