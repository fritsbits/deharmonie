# UX Strategy — Vrijwilligerspagina
_Status: Draft — open questions flagged below_

## Why this page exists

**80% of all activities at De Harmonie are run by volunteers.** Without them, most of the weekly program wouldn't exist. The volunteer page converts warm curiosity into a first contact — its only job is to make someone feel *invited*, then remove all friction from that first step.

**The distinctive angle:** De Harmonie doesn't just need logistics helpers. They genuinely welcome visitors who want to become creators — someone who comes to the Ciné-Club can become the one running it. This "participant → creator" arc is the emotional heart of the page.

## Primary audiences

| Audience | Mental state on arrival | What they need |
|----------|------------------------|----------------|
| **The semi-retired neighbor** (50-70) | Warm intent: "I want to give back, where do I start?" | Feel welcomed, see themselves reflected, understand what they'd actually do |
| **The active senior** (65-80) | Hopeful but uncertain: "Am I the right fit? Is it a big commitment?" | Reassurance about flexibility, low-pressure entry |
| **The student / young professional** (20-35) | Motivated by impact: "I want to do something meaningful locally" | Quick orientation, clear action step |
| **The referred visitor** (any age) | Word-of-mouth warm lead | Confirmation this is the right place, easy contact |

## Psychological reality

**Fears to dissolve:**
- Over-commitment: "What if I say yes and they need me every day?"
- Skill mismatch: "I don't have special skills, I'm just a regular person"
- Language barrier: "My Dutch/French isn't perfect"
- Being ignored: "Will they even respond if I reach out?"

**What the page must feel like:** A warm neighbor saying "we'd love to have you" — not an HR intake form or a corporate recruitment page.

**The shame barrier (from site strategy):** Visitors shouldn't feel like they're applying for a job or being screened. Volunteering at De Harmonie should feel like joining in, not applying.

## Org objectives

1. Attract volunteers who are reliable and genuinely connected to the community
2. Make the first contact step completely frictionless
3. Show the human side: volunteering here means real people, real community, real fun

## Tone (NL)

Informal `je/jou`, warm, verb-led. Examples:
- "Word deel van ons team" not "Vrijwilligersaanbod"
- "Je bepaalt zelf hoeveel tijd je geeft" not "Flexibele uren mogelijk"
- "Kom eens kennismaken" not "Neem contact op voor meer informatie"
