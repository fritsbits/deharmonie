# UX Strategy — Inschrijvingsflow

## Why this exists

The registration flow lets visitors claim a spot at an activity. It exists to give De Harmonie visibility into who is coming — not to gatekeep access.

## The mental model

**Registration = confirmed reservation by default.** The visitor gets a spot. Staff gets notified. Nothing more needs to happen unless something goes wrong.

This is not a "request awaiting approval." The tone of all touchpoints must reflect this.

## User needs

| Actor | What they need |
|-------|---------------|
| **Registrant** (senior or family member) | Quick confirmation that their spot is secured, key details to remember, nothing more required from them |
| **Staff** (animatie team, shared inbox) | To know who's coming, with enough info to reply if needed — handled from email, not admin |

## Psychology

- **Low anxiety** — visitors don't fear rejection; there's almost always space
- **Reassurance over process** — the confirmation should feel like a receipt, not a waiting room
- **No shame barrier** — the form is framed as practical, not as asking for help

## Organisational objective

Know who is coming. That's it. Not a CRM, not a workflow tool.

## Constraints

- Multiple staff share one inbox — no assignment needed, no complex workflow
- Low volume — keep everything simple, no queues, no reminders, no cancellations
- Admin is a light reference, not the primary tool — email is
