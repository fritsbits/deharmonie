# UX Strategy — De Harmonie (site-level)
_Status: Draft — to be validated with Cynthia_

## Why this website exists

De Harmonie is the community heart of the Noordwijk for 50 years. The website exists to make it findable, understandable, and inviting — primarily to the people who search on behalf of seniors: families and professional referrers.

**The hidden insight:** The people who USE De Harmonie and the people who FIND it online are often not the same people. The daughter searches; the mother visits. The social worker refers; the client comes. The site must serve the searchers while speaking to the visitors.

## User needs

| Audience | What they need from the site |
|----------|------------------------------|
| **Current visitors** (65-85+) | Weekly menu, activities, opening hours. Mostly offline — reached via family or print. |
| **Family members** (45-65) | Trust signals, clear service overview, first-visit info, pricing, contact. Searching on phone, often at night. |
| **Professional referrers** (OCMW, GPs, social workers) | Facts fast: service summary, eligibility, intake process, contact. |
| **Potential new visitors** (65-80) | Don't identify as "seniors needing help." Come for activities, not services. Non-institutional messaging. |
| **Institutional stakeholders** (VGC, funders) | Credibility: mission, team, impact data. |

## Site objectives

The website must do four things well:
1. **Answer the meal question** — What's on the menu this week? What does it cost? When can I come?
2. **Show what's happening** — Activity calendar that's current, inviting, and shareable
3. **Build trust for decision-makers** — Families and referrers need to feel confident recommending De Harmonie
4. **Be manageable by staff** — If the team can't update it easily, it will break again

## Positioning

**Shift:** From "a service center that helps seniors" → "the neighborhood heart of the Noordwijk where people meet, eat, learn, and look out for each other"

**The shame barrier:** 27% of non-attendees say "I don't feel old enough for a senior program." The site must never feel like a medical brochure or care catalog. Lead with community and activities.

## Tone

Warm, active, inclusive. Lead with verbs, not nouns.
- "Eet mee. Doe mee. Kom langs." — not "Diensten. Aanbod. Programma."
- "Bij ons ben je welkom" — not "Onze doelgroep is 65+"
- "Elke dinsdag leren we samen Italiaans" — not "Wekelijkse conversatietafel Italiaans"

## Menu pricing (from real menu PDF)

Standard meal (soup + main): **€9** most days, €10 for fish/special dishes.
Special event menus (e.g. Easter, Christmas): €20.
Saturday: usually closed, occasionally open.
This price point is a key accessibility signal — show it prominently.
