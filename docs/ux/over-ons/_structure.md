# UX Structure — Over ons (page-level)
_Status: Draft_

## Page role in the site

Over ons sits in the primary nav as a top-level item. Wie is wie is structurally its child (linked from within Over ons, and listed in the nav as a sub-item when on Over ons or Wie is wie).

```
Over ons
└── Wie is wie
```

This relationship is already reflected in the sitemap and nav. Over ons tells the organizational story; Wie is wie is the team directory.

## Page sections (top to bottom)

1. **Hero** — already built
2. **Mission story** — narrative prose, 2/3 + 1/3 layout with photo
3. **Photo strip** — full-bleed, 3 community photos
4. **Visitor voices** — 2–3 Google review quotes
5. **Team reference** — 1 sentence + link to Wie is wie
6. **CTA band** — blue background, "Kom langs" prompt

## Stage 2 insertion points

Impact numbers (from jaarverslag) can be inserted between the mission story and the photo strip — as a simple row of 3 large numbers with labels. This requires no layout change.

## User flows

**Family arriving from Google search "seniorencentrum Noordwijk Brussel":**
→ May land on homepage first, navigate to Over ons from nav
→ Reads mission story → sees photos → reads quotes → clicks through to Wie is wie or Contact

**Curious potential visitor (referred by neighbor):**
→ Lands on homepage, clicks "Over ons" from nav
→ Skims page for "does this look like my kind of place?"
→ Real photos and quotes do the work — confirms it's warm and normal
→ Clicks to activiteiten or contact

## Navigation role

Over ons is a "trust anchor" for the rest of the site. Families may check it before trusting the activiteiten or diensten content. It should feel like a natural stop in the journey, not a dead end.

The CTA at the bottom of Over ons points toward Contact — not activities. Families and referrers arriving here are in a "considering" state, not a "doing" state. The next step for them is to reach out, not to register for an activity.
