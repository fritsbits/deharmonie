# UX Strategy — Activiteiten (page-level)
_Status: Draft_

## Why this section exists

The activities section is the primary counter to the "shame barrier" (27% of non-attendees say they don't feel old enough for a senior program). It must never read as a clinical schedule or a care catalog. It exists to make people feel: **"Something's alive here. I'd fit in. I want to go."**

## The hidden asymmetry

The people who USE De Harmonie and the people who DISCOVER it online are often different. A daughter searches on her phone; her mother visits. The activities section must seduce the searcher while welcoming the visitor.

## User arrivals and mental states

| User | Mental state on arrival | Fear / barrier | Desire |
|------|------------------------|----------------|--------|
| **Curious outsider** (65–80, hasn't been yet) | Curious but guarded — doesn't identify as "needing senior services" | "Is this for old people? Will I feel out of place?" | To find something genuinely fun, to feel seen |
| **Family browser** (45–65, looking for their parent) | Scanning for fit — "would my mom enjoy this?" | "Is it depressing? Is it institutional?" | Variety, warmth, real photos of real people |
| **Regular visitor** | Task-oriented — checking if their class is still on | None | Logistics fast |
| **New regular** (has been once, considers committing) | Hopeful but not yet committed | "Is there enough here to keep coming back?" | Evidence of a rich, recurring program |

## What the page must feel like per user type

- **Curious outsider:** Warm, alive, unpretentious. Real people doing real things. Not a form. Not a list.
- **Family browser:** Breadth. "There's something here for her." Variety of types, not just dates.
- **Regular:** Scannable. Quick path to the full calendar.

## The core insight

> The overview page is **not a calendar**. It is an **invitation**.
> The calendar is a utility — it belongs on a separate page for people already convinced.

## Implications for the homepage activities section

The homepage activities section currently leads with date and reads like a calendar excerpt. It carries the same shame-barrier risk at smaller scale. It should be shifted to feel more like an invitation — lead with 2–3 special events as warm cards (photo + title + short description), not agenda rows. The "Bekijk alle activiteiten" link then points to this new invitation-first overview page.
