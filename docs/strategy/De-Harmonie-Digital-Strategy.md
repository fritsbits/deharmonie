## 1. Why This Strategy Exists

De Harmonie has been the beating heart of the Noordwijk for 50 years. The digital strategy exists to make sure the people who need De Harmonie can find it, understand it, and take the first step — whether that's coming in for a meal, signing up for an activity, or referring a client.

This is not a strategy about being "more digital." It's about using digital channels to serve the same mission De Harmonie has always had: helping people in the Noordwijk live well, stay connected, and remain at home.

---

## 2. Strategic Foundation

### 2.1 Organizational Mission (Woonzorgdecreet)

De Harmonie is legally mandated to:
1. Strengthen the self-care capacity and social networks of users
2. Strengthen social cohesion in the neighborhood
3. Support users to remain living at home independently
4. Contribute to local social policy

### 2.2 Digital Mission

**Make De Harmonie findable, understandable, and inviting to everyone who could benefit from it — visitors, their families, and the professionals who refer them.**

### 2.3 Core Problem We're Solving

1 in 5 elderly Brussels residents has no one to fall back on. Loneliness among 75+ year-olds has doubled since 2017. De Harmonie is one of the most effective interventions — but people can't use what they can't find.

The current digital presence has blind spots:
- The website's menu page is broken (404)
- 8 online registration requests went unanswered for 5 months
- De Harmonie is invisible in search results for home services
- Family members and referrers — the people who most often search online — find little that helps them take action

## 3. Who We're Reaching

### 3.1 Primary Audiences

| Audience | Size | Digital behavior | What they need |
|----------|------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Current visitors** (65-85+) | ~40-80 regulars | Low digital. Facebook sometimes. Mostly offline. | Weekly menu (printed or shared by family), activity schedule, opening hours |
| **Family members** (45-65) | Unknown but significant | High digital. Search on phone. Often at night or during work. | Trust signals, clear service overview, "first visit" info, pricing, contact |
| **Professional referrers** (OCMW, GPs, social workers) | Dozens in the Noordwijk network | Moderate digital. Bookmark useful pages. Need facts fast. | Service summary, contact, eligibility, intake process |
| **Potential new visitors** (65-80) | Large — many eligible, few aware | Low-moderate digital. Reached via family, neighbors, or Facebook | Welcoming, non-institutional messaging. Activities over services. |
| **Institutional stakeholders** (VGC, funders, partners) | Small but high impact | Check the website for credibility | Annual report, mission, team, impact data, professional presentation |

### 3.2 The Hidden Insight

The people who USE De Harmonie and the people who FIND De Harmonie online are often not the same people. The daughter searches, the mother visits. The social worker refers, the client comes. The digital strategy must serve the searchers while speaking to the visitors.

## 4. Channel Strategy

### 4.1 Channel Roles

| Channel | Role | Primary audience | Frequency |
|---------|------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Website** | Convince, inform, convert | Families, referrers, new visitors, funders | Updated weekly (menu, activities) |
| **Facebook** | Stay connected, share moments | Current visitors + their families | 2-3x per week |
| **Google Business Profile** | Get found locally | Everyone searching | Weekly (menu post, photos) |
| **Directory listings** (RESOO, sociaal.brussels, Born in Brussels) | Be present where people look | Referrers, families | Quarterly check for accuracy |
| **Email/WhatsApp** (future) | Direct communication with regulars | Current visitors + families | Weekly menu, monthly highlights |
| **Print** (flyers, menu on the wall, maandblad) | Reach non-digital audience | Current visitors, neighborhood | Weekly menu, monthly activity overview |

### 4.2 Website — The Hub

The website is the only channel De Harmonie fully controls. Every other channel should drive people to the website, and the website should make it effortless to take the next step.

**The website must do four things well:**
1. **Answer the meal question** — What's on the menu this week? What does it cost? When can I come?
2. **Show what's happening** — Activity calendar that's current, inviting, and shareable
3. **Build trust for decision-makers** — Families and referrers need to feel confident recommending De Harmonie
4. **Be manageable by staff** — If the team can't update it easily, it will break again

### 4.3 Facebook — The Community Board

Facebook is where De Harmonie's community already lives (519 followers). It should feel like a neighborhood bulletin board, not a corporate page.

**Content types that work:**
- Photos from today's activity (with permission)
- This week's menu as an image post (shareable)
- "Volgende week bij ons / La semaine prochaine chez nous" previews
- Event announcements for outings
- Occasional behind-the-scenes (the kitchen, preparations for a party)

**What to avoid:**
- Reposting the same content as the website verbatim
- Going silent for weeks (currently inconsistent)
- Overly formal language

**Realistic commitment:** 2-3 posts per week, done by the animation team (Deborah, Arnaud, Nicolas, Peter) as part of their existing workflow — photo after an activity, quick caption, post.

### 4.4 Google Business Profile — The Front Door

De Harmonie already has a strong Google listing (4.4 stars, 38 reviews). This is often the first thing people see.

**Actions:**
- Unify the address (Antwerpsesteenweg 24 — not Harmoniestraat 1)
- Post the weekly menu every Monday as a Google Business update
- Add recent photos of the space and activities (quarterly)
- Respond to reviews (even a simple "Bedankt!")
- Verify opening hours are correct
- Add attributes: wheelchair accessible, languages spoken, meal service

### 4.5 Directory Listings — The Safety Net

De Harmonie appears on RESOO, sociaal.brussels, Born in Brussels, Brussels Platform GGZ, and others. These are often how referrers find them.

**Actions:**
- Audit all listings for consistent name, address, phone, hours
- Ensure the website URL points to the new site after migration
- Add a 2-sentence description in NL and FR to each listing
- Check annually

## 5. Content Strategy

### 5.1 Content Pillars

All content across all channels should fit one of these four pillars:

| Pillar | Purpose | Examples |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **Samen aan tafel** (At the table together) | The social restaurant as entry point | Weekly menu, meal photos, pricing, delivery info |
| **Doe mee** (Join in) | Activities as community and growth | Activity calendar, workshop highlights, conversation tables, outings |
| **Bij u thuis** (At your home) | Home services as independence support | Service descriptions, Grote Kuis project, transport, cleaning |
| **Ons verhaal** (Our story) | Trust, credibility, community pride | Team introductions, 50-year heritage, annual report, neighborhood stories |

### 5.2 Content Calendar (Realistic)

| When | What | Who | Channel |
|------|------|-----|---------|
| **Monday morning** | Publish weekmenu | Nancy (admin panel) | Website → auto-generates print PDF |
| **Monday morning** | Post menu on Facebook + Google Business | Animation team | Facebook, Google Business |
| **After each activity** | Quick photo + caption | Person running the activity | Facebook |
| **Monthly** | Activity overview for next month | Animation team (admin panel) | Website |
| **Monthly** | Printed activity overview | Auto-generated from website | Print (in center + distributed) |
| **Quarterly** | Check directory listings | Coordinator | RESOO, sociaal.brussels, etc. |
| **Annually** | Jaarverslag / annual report | Coordinator + board | Website + PDF |

### 5.3 Tone of Voice

**Warm, active, inclusive.** De Harmonie speaks like a good neighbor — not like a government service.

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "Kom langs voor een maaltijd of een babbel" | "Het dienstencentrum biedt diensten aan" |
| "Elke dinsdag leren we samen Italiaans" | "Wekelijkse conversatietafel Italiaans" |
| "Bij ons ben je welkom" | "Onze doelgroep is 65+" |
| "Eet mee. Doe mee. Kom langs." | "Diensten. Aanbod. Programma." |

Lead with verbs. Speak in "we" and "together." Never make people feel like recipients of care — they're participants in a community.

## 6. Measurement Framework

### 6.1 What Success Looks Like

Success is not "more page views." Success is: **more people finding De Harmonie who need it, and taking the first step.**

### 6.2 Metrics

| Goal | Metric | How to measure | Target (Year 1) |
|------|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **People find us** | Monthly unique website visitors | Analytics (Plausible/Fathom — privacy-friendly) | Establish baseline, then +30% |
| **People find us** | Google Business views + actions | Google Business dashboard | Establish baseline |
| **People take action** | Activity registration requests | Laravel admin panel count | Track monthly |
| **People take action** | Phone calls mentioning the website | Staff tally (simple) | Qualitative |
| **Content stays current** | Menu page updated weekly | Admin panel last-updated date | 48/52 weeks |
| **Content stays current** | Activities published >2 weeks ahead | Admin panel check | Consistently |
| **Referrers use the site** | Professional page views | Analytics (URL tracking) | Establish baseline |
| **Funders see credibility** | Jaarverslag published online | Yes/No | Published by Q4 |

### 6.3 What NOT to Measure

- Don't obsess over Facebook likes or follower counts
- Don't track vanity metrics like "time on page"
- Don't compare to organizations with marketing budgets — De Harmonie is a 13-FTE community center, not a startup

## 7. Technical Infrastructure

### 7.1 Website Stack

| Component | Choice | Rationale |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Framework** | Laravel 11 | Flexibility, multilingual routing, Frederik's expertise |
| **Admin panel** | Filament 3 | Low-code admin for non-technical staff |
| **Frontend** | Tailwind CSS | Mobile-first, accessible, maintainable |
| **Database** | MySQL | Standard, reliable, affordable hosting |
| **Analytics** | Plausible or Fathom | Privacy-friendly (no cookies, GDPR-compliant) |
| **Hosting** | TBD | Belgian/EU hosting preferred for data residency |

### 7.2 Bilingual Architecture

URL-prefix routing: `/nl/` and `/fr/` with proper hreflang tags. Every public page exists in both languages. Admin panel in Dutch (primary staff language).

### 7.3 Key Admin Workflows

| Workflow | Who | Frequency | Admin feature |
|----------|-----|-----------|--------------|
| Update weekly menu | Nancy | Weekly | Simple form: date, soup, main, dessert, price (NL+FR) |
| Add/edit activities | Animation team | Weekly-monthly | Activity CRUD with bilingual tabs, recurring event support |
| Process registrations | Animation team | As they arrive | Dashboard with notification, one-click status update |
| Publish news/updates | Coordinator | Monthly | Simple blog-style editor |
| Update team page | Coordinator | As needed | Team member CRUD with photo upload |

## 8. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Staff don't update content | High | Site becomes stale (like now) | Make updates trivially easy in Filament. Build habits into existing routines (Monday = menu day). |
| Bilingual content bottleneck | Medium | FR content lags behind NL | Design admin to require both languages before publishing. Keep translations short and simple. |
| Low digital literacy of end users | High | Visitors don't use the website directly | Accept this. Design for the people who search on their behalf (families, referrers). Keep print as a parallel channel. |
| Scope creep | Medium | Project takes too long, costs too much | Start with the rebuild+improve scope. Add features (jaarverslag, donate, volunteer) as Phase 2. |
| Registration requests ignored again | High | Same problem as now | Email notification to animatie@deharmonie.be on every request. Weekly dashboard check as habit. |

## 10. Relationship to Website UX (Garrett Framework)

This digital strategy feeds directly into the website design using Jesse James Garrett's Elements of User Experience:

| Garrett Layer | What this strategy provides |
|--------------|----------------------------|
| **Strategy** | Site objectives (Section 2, 6), user needs (Section 3) |
| **Scope** | Feature requirements (Section 7.3), content requirements (Section 5), phased roadmap (Section 9) |
| **Structure** | Next step — information architecture, navigation, user flows |
| **Skeleton** | Next step — wireframes, page layouts, navigation design |
| **Surface** | Next step — visual design, typography, imagery |

The strategy is the roof. The Garrett layers are the walls and floors. We build top-down.
