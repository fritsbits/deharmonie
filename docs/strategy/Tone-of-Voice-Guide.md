# De Harmonie — Tone of Voice Guide
**Purpose:** Practical guide for anyone writing copy for De Harmonie's website, social media, or printed materials.
**Date:** March 25, 2026

---

## The One-Line Brief

Write like a warm neighbor who knows the Noordwijk well — not like an institution explaining its services.

---

## 1. Who We're Talking To (and How They Feel)

Every piece of copy should be written with one of these people in mind:

**The person who might come** — They're lonely but won't say it. They might feel "too young" for a dienstencentrum, or too proud to accept help. They need to feel invited, not served. They need to see themselves in what we show.

**The worried family member** — Searching at 10pm, feeling guilty they can't do more. They need clarity, reassurance, and trust — fast. They're not browsing; they're looking for answers.

**The professional referrer** — A social worker or GP with 60 cases. They need facts in seconds: what you do, where, when, for whom, how to refer.

**The curious neighbor** — Maybe retired, maybe just interested. Not "in need" of anything. Looking for something to do, people to meet. They'll bounce if the site feels like a medical brochure.

---

## 2. Voice Attributes

De Harmonie's voice has four qualities. Every piece of copy should have at least two of these present.

### Warm — not clinical
We sound like people who genuinely care, not like a policy document. We use first person plural ("we", "bij ons") and address the reader directly ("je", "u" depending on context — see language section).

| Instead of | Write |
|-----------|-------|
| Het dienstencentrum biedt diensten aan voor senioren | Bij ons kan je terecht voor een maaltijd, een activiteit, of gewoon een babbel |
| Le centre propose des services aux personnes âgées | Chez nous, vous êtes les bienvenus pour un repas, une activité, ou simplement un moment de convivialité |

### Grounded — not vague
We name real things: the Spanish conversation table on Thursday, Claude's dagschotel, the Grote Kuis project. Specificity builds credibility and makes abstract services tangible.

| Instead of | Write |
|-----------|-------|
| Diverse recreatieve activiteiten | Elke week: Spaanse conversatietafel, country line dance, naaiworkshop, zumba en meer |
| Nous offrons des activités variées | Chaque semaine : table de conversation en espagnol, danse country, atelier couture, zumba et bien plus |

### Inviting — not selling
We extend invitations, not pitches. The reader should feel welcome, not targeted. We never pressure. The most powerful word is "welkom."

| Instead of | Write |
|-----------|-------|
| Schrijf u nu in voor onze activiteiten | Kom gerust eens langs — je bent welkom |
| Inscrivez-vous à nos activités | Passez nous voir — vous êtes les bienvenus |

### Respectful — not patronizing
We never talk down, never use elderspeak, never imply the reader is helpless. We assume capability and intelligence. We frame support as mutual — not one-directional.

| Instead of | Write |
|-----------|-------|
| Wij helpen ouderen die het moeilijk hebben | Een buurt waar mensen naar elkaar omkijken |
| Nous aidons les personnes âgées en difficulté | Un quartier où les gens veillent les uns sur les autres |

## 3. Language Choices

### Words to use freely

| NL | FR | Why |
|----|-----|-----|
| welkom | bienvenue | Opens the door |
| samen | ensemble | Emphasizes togetherness |
| buurt, buurtbewoners | quartier, voisins | Grounds us in the neighborhood |
| bezoekers | visiteurs | Neutral, respectful |
| ontdek | découvrez | Active, curious |
| kom langs | passez nous voir | Low-threshold invitation |
| elke dag, elke week | chaque jour, chaque semaine | Rhythm and reliability |
| lekker, gezellig | convivial, chaleureux | Warmth and pleasure |

### Words to avoid or use sparingly

| Avoid | Why | Use instead |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| senioren (in headlines) | Triggers the "I'm not that old" barrier | buurtbewoners, bezoekers |
| hulpbehoevenden | Reduces people to their needs | mensen die thuis willen blijven wonen |
| doelgroep | Institutional jargon | de mensen die bij ons langskomen |
| cliënten, patiënten | Medical framing | bezoekers, deelnemers |
| aanbod | Sounds like a sales catalog | wat je bij ons kan doen |
| dienstverlening | Bureaucratic | hoe we je kunnen helpen |
| personnes âgées (in headlines) | Same barrier as "senioren" | habitants du quartier, visiteurs |
| bénéficiaires | Dehumanizing | les personnes qui nous rendent visite |
| prise en charge | Implies dependency | accompagnement, soutien |

### The "senioren" exception
In body text, service descriptions, and contexts where clarity is needed (SEO, referrer pages, legal/funding pages), "senioren" and "personnes âgées" are fine. The rule is: don't lead with them in headlines, hero sections, or emotional copy. A homepage that says "voor senioren" immediately tells half the potential audience "this isn't for me."

### Addressing the reader: je vs. u / tu vs. vous
**NL:** Use "je" as the default. It's warmer and more accessible. Reserve "u" for very formal contexts (official letters, subsidy documents).
**FR:** Use "vous" as the default — it's respectful without being cold. "Tu" would feel presumptuous.

## 4. Sentence Style

### Lead with verbs
The most powerful sentences start with what you can DO, not what we OFFER.

**Headlines and CTAs:**
- Eet mee. / Mangez avec nous.
- Doe mee. / Participez.
- Kom langs. / Passez nous voir.
- Leer bij. / Apprenez quelque chose de nouveau.
- Ontdek het weekmenu. / Découvrez le menu de la semaine.

### Short sentences for key messages
For the homepage, navigation labels, and CTAs: aim for 5-10 words. This audience includes people with reduced vision, cognitive decline, or limited language skills. Brevity is accessibility.

### Longer sentences for depth
Service descriptions, the "About us" page, and the "First visit" page can use longer, warmer prose — but keep paragraphs to 3-4 sentences maximum.

### Active voice always
| Passive (avoid) | Active (use) |
|----------------|--------------|
| Er worden activiteiten georganiseerd | Elke week organiseren we activiteiten |
| Des repas sont servis | Nous servons des repas chaque jour |
| U wordt geholpen door ons team | Ons team staat voor je klaar |

## 5. Writing for Bilingual Brussels

De Harmonie serves a neighborhood where people speak Dutch, French, Arabic, Turkish, Spanish, and more. The website will be fully bilingual NL/FR, but the tone should acknowledge this multilingual reality.

### Don't just translate — adapt
French copy shouldn't read like translated Dutch. Each language has its own rhythm and warmth.

**NL tends toward:** direct, compact, informal (je, gezellig, lekker)
**FR tends toward:** slightly more elaborate, courteous, descriptive (convivialité, chaleureusement, n'hésitez pas)

| NL | FR (not a word-for-word translation) |
|----|--------------------------------------|
| Kom gerust langs | N'hésitez pas à passer nous voir |
| Altijd welkom | Vous êtes toujours les bienvenus |
| Lekker eten, fijne mensen | Bonne cuisine, belles rencontres |

### Cultural sensitivity
The Noordwijk is multicultural. Our copy should:
- Never assume everyone shares the same cultural references
- Celebrate diversity naturally (through photos, team bios, multilingual conversation tables) without tokenizing
- Avoid assumptions about family structures, living situations, or beliefs

## 6. Tone by Page Type

### Homepage
**Tone:** Warm, inviting, alive. This is the front door — it should feel like walking into the center itself.
**Style:** Short emotional headline + three clear pathways. Lead with feeling, follow with function.
**Example NL:** "Al 50 jaar het warme hart van de Noordwijk. Kom eten, leren, bewegen en mensen ontmoeten."
**Example FR:** "Le coeur chaleureux du Noordwijk depuis 50 ans. Venez manger, apprendre, bouger et rencontrer."

### Weekly Menu
**Tone:** Practical but personal. This is the most-visited content — treat it like a daily invitation.
**Style:** Clean, scannable, with a touch of personality.
**Example:** "Maandag 31 maart — Tomatensoep / Kip met groenten en puree / Fruit — €X" (functional)
**Context line:** "Elke dag een verse, warme maaltijd. Samen aan tafel." (emotional frame)

### Activities
**Tone:** Energetic, specific. Each activity should feel like a personal invitation, not a calendar entry.
**Style:** Name + day/time + one sentence of what makes it special.
**Example NL:** "Spaanse conversatietafel — Elke donderdag van 10u tot 12u. Oefen je Spaans in een gezellige groep, van beginner tot gevorderde."
**Example FR:** "Table de conversation espagnole — Chaque jeudi de 10h à 12h. Pratiquez votre espagnol dans un groupe convivial, du débutant au confirmé."

### Services (diensten aan huis)
**Tone:** Reassuring, dignifying. These are for people who might feel vulnerable asking for help. Frame support as normal and neighborly.
**Style:** What it is + who it's for + how it works. Never "you need this" — always "this is here for you."
**Example NL:** "Boodschappendienst — Lukt boodschappen doen even niet zo goed? Onze medewerkers doen ze graag voor je. Bel ons en vertel wat je nodig hebt."
**Example FR:** "Service courses — Faire les courses est devenu compliqué ? Nos collaborateurs s'en chargent pour vous avec plaisir. Appelez-nous et dites-nous ce qu'il vous faut."

### About Us / Over Ons
**Tone:** Proud, grounded, human. This is where the 50-year story lives. Personal, not institutional.
**Style:** Narrative prose, not bullet points. Name real people. Show the building, the neighborhood, the history.
**Opening NL:** "Al meer dan 50 jaar is De Harmonie de plek waar de Noordwijk samenkomt. We begonnen in een oude veilinghal, en vandaag verwelkomen we elke dag tientallen buurtbewoners in ons vernieuwde centrum."
**Opening FR:** "Depuis plus de 50 ans, De Harmonie est le lieu de rencontre du Noordwijk. Nous avons commencé dans une ancienne salle de vente, et aujourd'hui nous accueillons chaque jour des dizaines d'habitants dans notre centre rénové."

### For Families (Voor Familie)
**Tone:** Empathetic, reassuring, direct. Acknowledge the worry. Offer clarity.
**Style:** Address the reader as the family member. Anticipate their questions. Be honest.
**Opening NL:** "Je maakt je zorgen over je vader, moeder of iemand anders die je dierbaar is. Dat begrijpen we. De Harmonie is een plek waar je naaste welkom is — voor een maaltijd, een activiteit, gezelschap. Geen instelling, geen verplichtingen. Gewoon een warme plek in de buurt."
**Opening FR:** "Vous vous inquiétez pour votre père, votre mère ou un proche. Nous comprenons. De Harmonie est un endroit où votre proche est bienvenu — pour un repas, une activité, de la compagnie. Pas d'institution, pas d'obligations. Simplement un lieu chaleureux dans le quartier."

### For Professionals (Voor Doorverwijzers)
**Tone:** Professional, efficient, trustworthy. Respect their time.
**Style:** Clear structure, scannable, with a one-paragraph summary at the top.
**Opening NL:** "De Harmonie is een erkend lokaal dienstencentrum in de Brusselse Noordwijk. We bieden dagelijks activiteiten, een sociaal restaurant en diensten aan huis voor ouderen en kwetsbare buurtbewoners. Doorverwijzen kan telefonisch of via het contactformulier."

## 7. Photography Direction

Photos are part of the voice. They should feel:
- **Real, not staged** — actual people at actual activities, not stock photos
- **Diverse** — reflecting the Noordwijk's multicultural reality
- **Active** — people doing things together (eating, talking, creating, dancing), not posed portraits
- **Warm** — natural light, candid moments, smiles that aren't forced
- **Dignifying** — never from above (looking down on subjects), always at eye level or below

**Always get photo consent** (the Airtable already has a "Fototoestemming" field for participants).

**Avoid:** Empty rooms, institutional-looking spaces, clipart, stock images of "happy seniors," images that reduce people to their age or vulnerability.

## 8. Quick Reference Card

For anyone writing copy — print this or keep it open while writing.

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Write "we" and "bij ons" | Write "het dienstencentrum" |
| Name specific activities | Say "diverse activiteiten" |
| Lead with verbs (Kom, Ontdek, Eet mee) | Lead with nouns (Aanbod, Diensten, Doelgroep) |
| Say "welkom" and "je bent welkom" | Say "schrijf u in" or "meld u aan" |
| Show real people doing real things | Use stock photos |
| Write short sentences for key messages | Write long bureaucratic sentences |
| Acknowledge multilingual reality | Assume everyone reads Dutch |
| Frame support as mutual/normal | Frame help as charity |
| Say "buurtbewoners" or "bezoekers" | Say "hulpbehoevenden" or "ouderen in nood" |
| Adapt FR copy (don't just translate NL) | Copy-paste through Google Translate |
