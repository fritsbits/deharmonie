⚠️ Design implication

At any given moment there are 0–1 upcoming special events. A "Binnenkort" section built only on upcoming specials will often be empty.

Proposal: The special events section should show a mix — upcoming specials (if any) + the last 2–3 recent ones as "what we did" evidence. This keeps the section alive year-round and doubles as social proof.
