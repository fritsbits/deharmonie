Design decisions baked in

  • Background: keeps the existing #eef2f8 blue-tint — cards pop off it naturally
  • Icons: fork+circle (Eten), group-of-people (Begeleiding), home (Thuis) — large, rotated, semi-transparent in the header
  • Colours: orange → green → blue, matching the brand palette already used across the site
  • Checkmarks: colour-matched to the card's accent colour (replacing the current always-orange ✓)
  • Border-radius 12px + shadow: matches the special activity cards exactly
  • Label split: "Eten &" in small muted text + "Activiteiten" as bold title — adds typographic rhythm to the header
